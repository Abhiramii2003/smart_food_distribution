How the Platform Works

A streamlined process to ensure food reaches those who need it most.

1. Submit Surplus

Restaurants quickly log their surplus food details, quantity, and event type.

2. Live Broadcast

The system broadcasts the surplus instantly to all verified NGOs in the local network.

3. Claim & Rescue

NGOs claim the food and rescue it, updating the real-time global impact tracker.
